{{ SITE_NAME }} is a community that connects blood donors with people who urgently need blood, across Bangladesh.

Our mission

When a family needs blood, minutes matter and phone calls go in circles. We want it to be easy to find willing donors nearby, easy to say "I can help", and easy to be thanked for it. Giving blood should feel like belonging to something good.

How it works

  1. Ask or answerPost an urgent request, or see who needs your blood group.
  2. Give at a blood bankDonations happen at hospitals and blood banks, with their screening.
  3. Log and get verifiedA volunteer checks each logged donation, so the numbers you see are real.

How it is run

{{ SITE_NAME }} is run by volunteers. It is free to use, and has no ads. Staff approve donations, review reports and look after the data. We are a community tool, not a hospital or a medical service.

How privacy works

Your number stays private

Phone numbers never appear on public pages. A donor shares theirs only after accepting a contact request.

You choose what is visible

Directory listing is opt-in. The leaderboard and badge card can be switched off any time in Profile & privacy.

Names, not numbers

Public pages show a name you gave, never your login.

Safety principles

The blood bank decides

Nothing here is medical clearance. Eligibility reminders and recovery gaps are prompts to check with the blood bank.

Voluntary, never for sale

Blood is given freely. Badges and points celebrate giving; they are never a reason to donate when you should rest.

Report anything that feels off

Reported requests are hidden until staff have looked at them.
