Before you give

Can I donate?

Who

Healthy adults aged 18 to 60 who weigh at least 45 kg. The blood bank checks haemoglobin and health on the day.

How often

About every {{ donation_interval_label }}*. We show a recovery bar after each donation so you know when you are ready.

How safe

Sterile single-use equipment, and a screening before every donation. You can always say no.

* A general reminder, not medical advice. The blood bank's screening decides your eligibility. Read the full guide.
