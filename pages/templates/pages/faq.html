Privacy

Is my phone number public?

No. Your number is never shown on public pages, in the leaderboard or on share cards. It is only your login. Other members see it only if you accept their contact request.

What can other people see about me?

Your name (or "Donor #" and a number if you gave none), tier and donation count appear on the leaderboard if you allow it. If you join the donor directory, logged-in members also see your blood group, district and whether you are available.

Change any of this in Profile & privacy.

Can I stay off the leaderboard?

Yes. Turn off "Show me on the leaderboard and home page". Your donations still count in the community totals, without your name.

Who sees my badge card?

Anyone with the link, if you leave sharing on. Turn it off and only you and site staff can open it.

Verification

How are donations verified?

You log a donation with the date and place. A site volunteer reviews it and approves or rejects it. Only approved donations count towards points, tiers, the leaderboard and community numbers.

Why is my donation still pending?

Someone has to check it by hand, which can take a little while. Meanwhile it still starts your recovery gap, so the app will not nudge you to give again too soon.

What if a donation is rejected?

It does not count, and it no longer blocks you. Staff may leave a note explaining why. You can log it again with correct details.

Phone numbers and contact

How do I contact a donor?

Find them in the donor directory and send a short request. The donor sees your message and chooses to accept or decline. Only if they accept do they share their number with you.

Who sees the contact number on a blood request?

The person asking for blood adds a number so donors can reach them. It is shown only to logged-in members, and only while the request is still active.

Someone is bothering me. What can I do?

Decline their request and do not reply. Report suspicious requests using the report button on the request page. Several reports hide a request until staff have checked it.

Points and badges

How do I earn points?

Each approved donation earns points. You earn a bonus when a donation answers a blood request, and for keeping a steady streak of donations.

What are tiers and badges?

Tiers (Bronze up to Platinum) reflect how many verified donations you have given. Other badges mark things like helping on requests and keeping a streak. See yours on your hub.

Is this a competition?

Only a friendly one. Giving blood too often is not safe, so waiting out your recovery gap never costs you a streak by itself. The gap we show is a reminder, not medical advice.

Blood requests

How do I ask for blood?

Log in and post a request with the blood group, hospital, district, how many bags and the date needed. Donors with a matching group can answer with "I can help".

Does "I can help" mean a donation?

No. It tells the requester someone may come. A donation counts only after it is logged and verified.

When does a request close?

When the date passes, when enough verified donations cover the bags needed, or when the requester closes it. Please close it as soon as you have the blood.

Is this a replacement for the blood bank?

No. {{ SITE_NAME }} helps people find each other. In an emergency, contact the hospital's blood bank directly. The blood bank screens every donor and decides who can give.
